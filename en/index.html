About me

More than technology — understanding the problem and taking the work through to a result.

My strength is the combination of technical thinking, writing, information structuring, data work and communication. In many projects I work end-to-end — from the idea, content and digital environment to client communication, execution, post-processing and documentation.

I work both independently and as part of a team. I learn continuously, test, analyse mistakes and build the next iteration. I can break work into stages, set priorities and align the solution so the final result meets the real need — not just the original idea.
